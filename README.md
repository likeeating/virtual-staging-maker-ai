# Virtual Staging Maker: Furnish photos of empty rooms with AI

Listing photos of empty rooms can be hard to picture as a finished space. [https://virtualstagingmaker.com/](https://virtualstagingmaker.com/) is a virtual staging AI from our team at Deep Origin. It furnishes photos of empty rooms in 12 styles. Upload a listing photo, press Generate and download it in 2K or 4K.

## What it does

- Furnishes a photo of an empty room in the style you choose
- 12 styles to pick from
- Choose a room type
- Download a 2K or 4K photo for your listing

## How to stage a listing photo

1. Upload a photo of an empty room.
2. Pick a room type and one of the 12 styles.
3. Press Generate.
4. Download the 2K or 4K photo for your listing.
